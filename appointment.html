Frequently asked questions

How long is a typical appointment?

Most first visits take around 30–45 minutes. Follow-up visits may be shorter.

Can I request a specific appointment time?

Yes. Choose your preferred date and time range and the clinic will confirm availability.

What if I need to cancel?

Call the clinic as soon as possible so the appointment can be released to another patient.
